# Sponsor Not Sponsor — handoff

Updated: October 6, 2026 (America/Chicago)

## Current product behavior

The extension scans the current vacancy and produces one of four headlines: `Explicit blocker found`, `Sponsorship stated`, `No blocker found`, or `Could not verify`. Silence does not prove sponsorship or refusal. Application questions do not establish employer policy. Citizenship, U.S.-person, export-control, CPT, and OPT restrictions remain distinct facts even when they affect the final blocker headline.

The active scope is the named vacancy. Policies solely about another job or later full-time conversion do not determine this vacancy. A requirement imposed on applicants to this vacancy that they need no sponsorship “now or in the future” still applies.

## Separate deterministic and probabilistic results

The popup and expanded page panel now show two independent results:

- `Deterministic result` comes from the local text interpreter and remains the compact badge result.
- `Probabilistic result · Laya` comes from the opt-in local model and has its own state, interpretation, explanation, and citations.

Laya's comparison no longer inherits rule-derived sponsorship refusals or restrictions. Its current contract covers scope, sponsorship, timing, CPT, and OPT. It does not classify citizenship, work authorization, export controls, or other restriction types, and the UI states that limitation. Disabled, pending, invalid, and unavailable Laya states leave the deterministic result visible. Downloaded reports include both outputs.

The implementation plan and completed validation are recorded in [DECISION_COMPARISON_PLAN.md](DECISION_COMPARISON_PLAN.md). Validation passed: TypeScript typecheck, 212 unit tests, production build, mocked browser integration, and `git diff --check`. The browser mock deliberately produced a disagreement—rules found a blocker while Laya stated sponsorship—and verified both popup and page-panel results, opt-out, service outage, stale-navigation handling, request privacy, and the deterministic badge.

## Fine-tuning status and next work

Research and initialization are complete, including a real MPS optimizer smoke update. Domain fine-tuning has not started because the current five-question records are only partially reviewed and lack independent calibration/test groups.

Next steps:

1. Review scope, timing, CPT, and OPT for the 13 passages, or explicitly reduce the first training contract before producing training targets.
2. Collect more varied, source-backed examples, especially explicit sponsorship offers and conditional cases.
3. Create employer/vacancy/policy-family-disjoint training, calibration, and held-out test groups. Do not use the exposed 54-job roster as unseen test data.
4. Extend the smoke runner into a multi-epoch domain trainer with split inputs, scheduler, calibration, and resumable optimizer state.
5. Compare deterministic, base Laya, tuned Laya, and any hybrid on identical inputs. Keep extraction failures separate from interpretation errors.
6. Promote no checkpoint until the frozen evaluation supports it.
