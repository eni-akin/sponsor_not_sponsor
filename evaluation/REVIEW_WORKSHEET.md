## What you will do

Review these 12 postings in order. Do the first six before moving to the second six.

For each posting:

1. Open the official job link.
2. Confirm that it is still open. If it is closed or redirected to a general careers page, record that and stop reviewing that posting.
3. Read the posting yourself before opening the extension’s evidence panel.
4. Decide whether the posting has an explicit international-applicant blocker, explicitly states sponsorship, or contains neither.
5. Copy the exact sentence supporting a blocker or an explicit sponsorship statement.
6. Open the extension panel and record what the extension reported.
7. Note whether the extension found the correct job title and whether its answer agrees with yours.

A sponsorship question on an application is not a blocker. “Must be authorized to work” does not automatically mean “no sponsorship.” Do not use company reputation or the Simplify label as the answer; judge the official posting’s words.

Use [the plan's source rules](../PLAN.md#evidence-and-source-rules) when wording is uncertain. The official posting controls this review; company policies and historical records stay separate.
