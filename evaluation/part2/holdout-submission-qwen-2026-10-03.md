### General Logic & Classification Rules for Sponsorship Determination
To ensure consistency and transparency, the following explicit logic rules were applied to determine the sponsorship status of each job posting based **strictly on the extracted public text**:

1. **Explicit Denial**: The text explicitly states the company "will not sponsor", "does not offer visa sponsorship", or requires the candidate to be "authorized to work without sponsorship now or in the future." *(Classification: No Sponsorship)*
2. **Regulatory / Clearance Mandate**: The text requires "U.S. Citizenship", "U.S. Person" status (as defined by ITAR/EAR export controls), or the "ability to obtain a U.S. security clearance" (e.g., Secret, TS/SCI). Because these are legal prerequisites that cannot be sponsored, this functionally prohibits sponsorship for non-citizens/non-LPRs. *(Classification: No Sponsorship / Restricted)*
3. **No Blockers Found (Application Screen Only)**: The public job description lacks an explicit denial of sponsorship. While the application form may include standard compliance questions (e.g., "Will you require sponsorship?", "Are you legally authorized to work?"), these are standard data-gathering practices and do not inherently constitute a hard "No" or blocker. Without an explicit denial in the public text, sponsorship is not explicitly ruled out. *(Classification: No blockers found)*
4. **Not Mentioned / Gap**: The extracted text (both job description and visible application fields) contains absolutely no mention of work authorization, visas, citizenship, or sponsorship. *(Classification: Not Mentioned)*
