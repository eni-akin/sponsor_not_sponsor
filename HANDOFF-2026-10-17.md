### Adaptive on-page placement

Both the badge and evidence panel assess four corners and choose a clear one, preferring bottom-right. If all corners are obstructed, they choose the least-obstructed corner. Document mutations, UI resize, scroll/viewport resize, and a 500 ms visible-tab fallback cover late and moving overlays.

The owner reported successful placement testing. Agent-run tests used local synthetic helpers, including closed-shadow and iframe overlays. Simplify was disabled when inspected; the agent did not enable it or change its settings. The owner's report is not a new independent live audit.

Placement uses nine probes per corner: thin overlays can evade sampling. Chrome toolbar popups and other application windows are outside its control. Existing iframe invalidation can close an evidence panel when the current role becomes unsafe to retain; preserve that protection.

### Final pill/glow behaviour

The floating badge is a pill with a soft, static ember underglow matching **Scan again**. The glow and on-page primary buttons share `--sns-accent: #C94F32` in [src/page-ui.css](src/page-ui.css).

On first visible arrival for the current role, an SVG highlight makes one complete perimeter circuit over **1.4 seconds**, then fades out. Moving corners, ordinary result updates, and closing the evidence panel do not replay it. Reduced motion skips the sweep; reduced-motion/forced-colour styles hide the decorative tracer. It cannot intercept clicks or keyboard focus.

The final request superseded website-matched glow and the original 2.4-second duration. The website-theme sampler, colour probe, and theme listener were removed. Keep the fixed SNS palette unless the owner requests another change. Sponsorship logic, result colours, and evidence-panel layout were not changed by this feature.
