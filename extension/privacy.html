Privacy and data handling

Local preview · Updated September 28, 2026

This preview reads page text in your browser to explain job requirements. Company research is not active in this extension. There are no analytics, ads, or accounts. Optional Local Laya runs AI inference on your computer.

Automatic page access

The extension has access to HTTP and HTTPS pages to recognize jobs automatically, including links from different sources. It inspects visible page text and job metadata in the top-level document. Standard form values, editable answers, file uploads, and hidden content are excluded. Page text can itself contain personal information; exclusion rules are not a guarantee about everything a website displays.

Local scan text and findings stay in tab memory and are replaced as you navigate or rescan. They are not automatically sent to the developer or saved as a browsing history. Enabling Local Laya additionally sends extracted public job passages to the local decision service described below. You can pause scanning everywhere or disable a site. Chrome’s own site-access controls can further restrict access.

Saved settings and optional reports

Chrome local storage saves scanning preferences, disabled site names, the Local Laya setting, and whether onboarding was dismissed. A research preference saved by an older version may remain stored but is ignored. If you explicitly download an incorrect-result report, it includes role details, findings, quotations, scan time, and the page address with query and fragment removed. It excludes the full extraction and form answers. Reports stay wherever you save them; nothing is automatically submitted.

When you enable Local Laya

The extension sends extracted job passages, job title and employer to http://127.0.0.1:4319 on this computer. Standard application answers and uploads are excluded by the scanner. The model judges sponsorship, CPT and OPT; requirement highlights still use local rules.

Setup downloads open model weights and dependencies. The decision service forces offline inference and does not send job text to a model provider. It keeps up to 10 decisions in memory for up to 15 minutes, removing expired entries on later requests. It does not write job text or decisions to disk. Turning Local Laya off stops new requests and hides model results; in-flight inference can finish. Stop the service to clear its memory. Model weights remain in .model-cache until you delete them.

Company research

Company research is deferred and inactive in this extension. It does not send employer or role details to a research service, fetch company policy pages, or send search queries to a provider. Research code left from earlier versions is not used by the active extension. A cache created by a separately run older service remains on disk until you remove that service’s configured cache directory. Uninstalling removes extension storage; downloaded reports and model files remain where saved.

Use of information

This build uses the described information only for job analysis, optional local model decisions, settings, and reports you choose to download. It contains no sale, advertising, analytics, or unrelated transfer mechanism. Future changes to data handling require updated disclosures.
