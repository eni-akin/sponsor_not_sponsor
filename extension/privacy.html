Privacy and data handling

Local copy · Updated October 30, 2026

This extension reads page text in your browser to explain job requirements using deterministic rules. Company research is not active in this extension. There are no analytics, ads, or accounts.

Automatic page access

The extension has access to HTTP and HTTPS pages to recognize jobs automatically, including links from different sources. It inspects visible page text and job metadata in the top-level document. Standard form values, editable answers, file uploads, and hidden content are excluded. Page text can itself contain personal information; exclusion rules are not a guarantee about everything a website displays.

Local scan text and findings stay in tab memory and are replaced as you navigate or rescan. They are not automatically sent to the developer or saved as a browsing history. You can pause scanning everywhere or disable a site. Chrome’s own site-access controls can further restrict access. A narrowly matched Ashby application may fetch its official job overview from the same website to recover a missing description; the extension does not send the extracted text to an analysis service.

Saved settings and optional reports

Chrome local storage saves scanning preferences, disabled site names, and whether onboarding was dismissed. Preferences saved for removed features may remain stored but are ignored. If you explicitly download an incorrect-result report, it includes role details, findings, quotations, scan time, and the page address with query and fragment removed. It excludes the full extraction and form answers. The file stays wherever you save it. Opening the separate reporting form sends no report or job details, though the visit makes an ordinary web request. You must import and review the file, then press Submit report to send it.

A submitted report can include the posting URL, title, employer, location, correction category and explanation, expected outcome, relevant public passage, and reviewed diagnostic fields. You may separately provide an email address for manual follow-up; no automatic email is sent. Eniola receives reports in a private inbox using Cloudflare for processing and storage. Temporary network information is processed to limit abuse. Reports and separate contact details are deleted within a day of the earlier of 180 days after receipt or 90 days after closure. Temporary recovery copies and Cloudflare operational metadata may persist under provider practices. Sanitized confirmed issues may remain in GitHub without reporter identity. To request deletion, contact eni.akinpel@gmail.com; include your receipt, which helps locate a report but does not by itself prove ownership.

Company research

Company research is deferred and inactive in this extension. It does not send employer or role details to a research service, fetch company policy pages, or send search queries to a provider. Research code left from earlier versions is not used by the active extension. A cache created by a separately run older service remains on disk until you remove that service’s configured cache directory. Uninstalling removes extension storage; downloaded reports remain where saved.

Use of information

This build uses the described information for local job analysis, settings, and reports you explicitly choose to submit. It contains no sale, advertising, analytics, or unrelated transfer mechanism. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Future changes to data handling require updated disclosures.
