Read the evidence.

A guide to the preview and its limits.

Start with a single job

Open or reload a job posting. When the extension recognizes one role, a small badge appears. Click it to see the main result and its supporting quotations.

Use the toolbar popup for Scan again, Show on page, pause, and site controls. The badge’s × hides it for the current role until reload; Show on page restores it. Escape closes the evidence panel.

The badge and evidence panel prefer the bottom-right corner and can move around floating page tools. They also check for overlays that appear or move after loading. If every corner is occupied, SNS uses the least-obstructed one; use the toolbar popup if needed. Chrome's own toolbar popup and other application windows are outside this positioning system.

The pill-shaped badge has a soft SNS ember glow matching the Scan again button and one 1.4-second arrival sweep around its edge. Website colours do not change the glow. Reduced-motion preferences disable the sweep. The glow is decorative and does not change the meaning or colours of the result.

Understand the result

  • Explicit blocker found: recognized wording refuses sponsorship, excludes CPT/OPT, or states a mandatory eligibility restriction. Read the affected group, timing, and exceptions.
  • Sponsorship stated: the posting offers or considers sponsorship, with no overriding blocker. Read the conditions.
  • No blocker found: the complete posting has no recognized blocker or sponsorship statement. This does not prove sponsorship is available.
  • Could not verify: evidence is incomplete, conflicting, or needs review.

Application questions do not establish employer policy. CPT and OPT each need their own explicit evidence; a sponsorship refusal alone establishes neither. Citizenship and other eligibility restrictions remain distinct from sponsorship policy. Findings describe source wording, not your personal eligibility.

If a job is missed

Wait for the description to load, select a single vacancy, then try Scan again. Check that scanning is not paused or disabled for the site and that Chrome permits access. After updating the extension, reload the job tab.

The preview uses a limited set of English page and phrase rules. It can miss unusual wording, employers without readable metadata, jobs inside embedded frames, PDFs, and unsupported layouts. It does not carry findings from a posting to a separate application. It has not completed independent real-world accuracy validation.

Company research

Company research is deferred and is not available in the active extension. The extension does not look up employer policies or send role details to a research service.

Report an incorrect result

In the evidence panel, choose Report incorrect result, select the issue, and download the report. Nothing is sent automatically. Review the JSON before sharing: it contains the role, findings, quoted evidence, and a page address without query or fragment. The preview has no automatic support inbox.
