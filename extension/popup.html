A quick introduction

Website access lets the extension recognize jobs automatically. A small badge appears for a detected role; click it for the evidence.

Page scanning stays in your browser. Application answers and uploads are excluded. Company research starts off; you can enable it below after reading what it shares.

You can dismiss a badge for the current role, pause scanning, or disable it for a site. If a badge is dismissed or cannot fit safely, use “Show on page” below.

    Company research settings

    Optional: check official sources when a finding is unclear. Requires the local research service.

    Enabling sends the employer, job title, role ID, location, and page address without its query or fragment to the service on this computer. The service visits official sites and, if configured, sends search terms to Brave Search. It never receives page text, answers, or uploads. Research details and public-source excerpts are cached locally and reused for up to 24 hours; expired files are removed on later requests.

    Page scanning stays local. Optional company research shares only the role details described in its settings. Preferences are saved locally.
