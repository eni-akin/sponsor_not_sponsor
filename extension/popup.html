A quick introduction

Website access lets the extension recognize jobs automatically. A small badge appears for a detected role; click it for the evidence.

Scanning stays in your browser. Application answers and uploaded files are excluded. Only pause and site preferences are saved, along with this welcome screen’s status. Company research is not enabled.

You can dismiss a badge for the current role, pause scanning, or disable it for a site. If a badge is dismissed or cannot fit safely, use “Show on page” below.
