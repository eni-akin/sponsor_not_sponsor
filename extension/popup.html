A quick introduction

Website access lets the extension recognize jobs automatically. A small badge appears for a detected role; click it for the evidence.

Page scanning stays in your browser. Application answers and uploads are excluded. Optional Local Laya sends job passages to a decision service on this computer when enabled below.

You can dismiss a badge for the current role, pause scanning, or disable it for a site. If a badge is dismissed or cannot fit safely, use “Show on page” below.

    Decision engine · Local Laya

    Compare the deterministic result with a separate local model result for sponsorship, CPT and OPT. The compact badge continues to use deterministic rules.

    Enabling sends the extracted job passages, job title and employer to the decision service on this computer. Application answers and uploads are excluded. Inference runs offline after model setup. Job text is not written to disk; decisions are cached in service memory for up to 15 minutes. Turning this off stops new requests; an already-started request may finish.

    Requires the local decision service. Pending, unavailable and disabled states are shown beside the deterministic result. Laya does not evaluate citizenship, work authorization, export controls or other rule restrictions. This preview has not established real-world accuracy.
