Before contacting support

  • Wait for the job description to load, select one vacancy, then choose Scan again.
  • Check that scanning is not paused or disabled for the site and that Chrome allows site access.
  • After updating the extension, reload the job tab.

Report an incorrect result

In the evidence panel, choose Report incorrect result, select the issue, and Download report. The JSON stays local and includes role details, findings, quoted evidence, scan time, and a sanitized page address; it excludes the full page extraction and form answers. Choose Open reporting form to visit the separate site. Downloading or opening the form sends no report or job details, though the visit makes an ordinary web request. Import and review the file there, then press Submit report to send it to Eniola. If the form is paused or unavailable, keep the local file and use the support email above. For a deletion request, include your receipt to help locate the report; a receipt alone does not prove ownership.

Known limits

The extension targets English-language U.S. job postings. It can miss unusual wording, employers without readable metadata, inaccessible pages, PDFs, and unsupported layouts. A result describes the posting's recognized wording; it is not legal advice, a personal eligibility decision, or proof that sponsorship is available.
