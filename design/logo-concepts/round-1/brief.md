# SNS — logo concept round 1

Status: exploratory concepts, not approved branding. Existing extension icons are unchanged.

## Approved brief

SNS (Sponsor Not Sponsor) helps international students inspect job-posting wording. The identity should feel professional but playful, bold but relaxed; familiar enough to approach and distinctive enough to spot. Duolingo is a reference for warmth and recognition, not for its owl, geometry, or palette. Start completely fresh. Intended uses: Chrome toolbar and GitHub README. Colour is open; establish the silhouette in black first. Do not imply guaranteed sponsorship, legal advice, or official approval.

## Strategy and reference study

Bundled logo-library study: education and developer-tools category summaries; Ello, GNOME, Discord, and Twitch geometry. These are study references, not source artwork. Faces can work with very few features; thick negative spaces matter more than detail. Category clichés to avoid: document sheets, shields, approval ticks, magnifiers, globes, graduation caps, and generic blue technology badges. An owl would be too close to the user's inspiration.

Word map: SNS → repeated S / contrast / paired readings; evidence → quotation / opening / revealing; international students → discovery / companionship / possibility; relaxed + bold → generous curves / solid silhouettes / few features.

Selected: Peek, Name First, Between. They test personality, explicit naming, and compact abstraction rather than three decorative variations of one idea. Peek is the initial recommendation because it best addresses the user's playful-but-professional preference. Risks: Peek's eyes may be lost when tiny; Name First is wide and the letters may not be individually readable at 16 px; Between needs the SNS name nearby while recognition develops.

## Construction before drawing

- A / Peek: one closed S silhouette on a 256-unit canvas, curved shoulders and generous weight; two capsule-shaped negative-space eyes. The S is the primary identifier and the face is the second reading.
- B / Name First: three custom filled letters, no font; a 20-unit stem, 24-unit corner family, and a central N with a diagonal bridge. Deliberately typographic rather than a character.
- C / Between: two opposing 80-unit-radius quotation corners, with 40-unit weight and a generous diagonal channel; both pieces are exact rotational counterparts. The open centre suggests looking between statements, not a yes/no approval badge.

SVG versions retain iterations. Presentation-sheet labels use system fonts only; concept artwork has no live text or external fonts. No final font selection, colour system, brand kit, or production icon exports are included before direction approval.

## Checks and refinements

Two visual/structural passes completed. SVGs and HTML test sheets were rendered locally in isolated temporary browser profiles; the user's open Chrome session was not used or changed. The overview and both iteration test sheets were inspected, along with full second-pass sections for B/C and the library-reference shelf.

- A v2: centred the silhouette, separated the eye holes from the mouth opening, increased eye width, and resolved near-miss straight-edge angles. The S survives at 16 px; the face is clearer at 24–32 px. A dedicated tiny-size cut remains a final-kit task, not a claim that the expression is fully readable at 16 px.
- B v2: smoothed the S transitions and increased cap height for more presence. Reads clearly at normal README sizes and 32 px; at 16 px, three condensed letters are the weakest option. All lettering was drawn as filled vector paths, not set in a stock font.
- C v2: introduced matching 45-degree cut terminals. The broad opposing shapes survive at 16 px, but their connection to SNS is learned rather than explicit. Rotational symmetry is deliberate.
- Inspected one-colour, white reversed, blur, mirror/rotation, avatar and browser-tab contexts, and comparison against four library references. Original black artwork disappears on dark backgrounds: use a reversed treatment or contrasting tile; do not deploy the black concept unchanged in dark mode. Reversed small-size optical tuning is deferred to the approved kit.
- The audit script reports no structural issues for all three v2 SVGs (100/100 heuristic scores). This is not a measure of originality, user recognition, legal clearance, or production approval.

No independent user-recognition study, trademark search, reverse-image search, final colour check, extension installation, or print-production proof was performed. These are review concepts, not launch-ready assets. Next: user chooses a direction or requests revisions; prepare the full kit only with approval.
